Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Easyy AI ("Easyy", "we", "us") collects when you use Easyy AI Job Alerts (the "Service"), why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run your job alerts.

1. Data we collect

DataWhy we collect it
NameTo personalize your alerts and communications.
Email addressTo sign you in and to deliver job digests and account emails.
Mobile number & countryTo contact you about your account and, if offered, send alerts; the country sets sensible search defaults.
Search preferencesThe job titles, locations, salary, and filters you configure — needed to find your matches.
Telegram chat IDOnly if you connect Telegram, so we can send alerts to your chat.
Basic usage dataTo keep the Service secure and working.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the job searches you set up and deliver matching results by email and Telegram.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To respond to support requests and send essential service notices.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that make Easyy work, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Supabase — stores your account and preferences (our database and authentication).
  • Resend — sends account and digest emails on our behalf.
  • Telegram — delivers alerts, only if you connect it.
  • Job sources (LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Jobs) — we send them your search terms to find listings; we don't share your identity with them.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your searches or your entire account at any time from your settings; when you delete your account, we remove your personal data from our systems, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations. You can also email us to request deletion.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our database provider using row-level security so that you can only access your own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use only what's necessary to keep you signed in (a session stored by your browser). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these directly in your settings, or by contacting us. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where that happens, we rely on providers that offer appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you.

Privacy questions or requests? Contact us at support@easyy-ai.com.
