Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Easyy ("Easyy", "we", "us") collects when you use Easyy Job Alerts and the Easyy Resume builder (together, the "Service"), why we collect it, who else sees it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to find jobs for you and tell you about them.

Why we ask for your mobile number

A mobile number is required to create an account. We use it for three things:

  • One person, one account. The Service is free and runs real searches against job sites every hour; requiring a number lets us keep the Service fair and stop automated sign-ups.
  • Reaching you about your account. If your alerts stop working — for example your email bounces or Telegram disconnects — a number gives us a second way to tell you.
  • Alerts by SMS or WhatsApp, when we offer them. We will ask before turning any new channel on; adding a number does not opt you in.

We never sell your number, never share it with employers or job sites, and never use it for marketing. In the app your number is shown with the middle digits masked.

Your resume content

If you use the Resume builder you may give us your master resume (work history, education, skills, contact details) and job descriptions you paste or pick from your alerts. We use this content for one purpose: to produce tailored resumes and related drafts (such as a referral note) for you, on your request.

  • Resume content is processed by an AI model provider on our behalf. We choose providers whose commercial terms prohibit them from training their models on our customers' content. (Lawyer: confirm the named provider and DPA before launch.)
  • We keep your master resume and the tailored versions so you can reuse and download them. You can delete any of them, or all of them, at any time in the app.
  • Tailored resumes can contain mistakes. Always review a document before you send it to an employer.
  • Resume content may include sensitive details you choose to write (nationality, date of birth, photo). We recommend leaving those out; we do not require or use them.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the job searches you set up and deliver matching results by email and, if connected, Telegram.
  • To keep an accurate History of what we sent you and avoid repeat alerts.
  • To produce tailored resumes when you ask for them.
  • To authenticate you, secure your account, and respond to support requests.
  • To send essential service notices (for example a change to these terms). We do not send marketing emails.

We do not sell personal data, share it with advertisers, or build profiles of you for anyone else. Where the law asks for a legal basis: running your searches, storing your history and tailoring resumes are necessary to provide the Service you asked for; security and abuse prevention are our legitimate interest; anything optional (Telegram, SMS/WhatsApp alerts) runs on your consent, which you can withdraw in the app.

3. Who we share it with

Only the providers that make Easyy work, each under its own security and privacy terms and only for the purpose listed:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication (your account, settings, matches, alert history, resumes).
  • Resend — sends account emails such as sign-in links and password resets.
  • Brevo — sends your job-alert emails and daily digests.
  • Telegram — delivers alerts to your chat, only if you connect it.
  • AI model provider — processes resume content to produce tailored resumes, only if you use the Resume builder.
  • Job sources (LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Jobs) — we send them your search terms and location to find listings. We never send them your name, email, number or resume.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of Easyy or our users.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, settings, resumes: for as long as your account exists.
  • Job matches and alert history: up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Job postings themselves (title, company, link) are public listings and are not personal data; we keep them briefly for de-duplication.
  • Authentication logs: retained by our provider for a limited period for security.

When you delete your account we remove your profile, searches, matches, alert history, Telegram link and resumes from our systems. Telegram alerts stop immediately. We may keep the minimum records the law requires (for example, that a deletion request was received).

5. Deleting your account and other rights

You can delete your searches, your resumes, or your entire account from Profile & settings in the app, or by emailing support@easyy-ai.com from your account address. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict your data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). It is stored with our database provider using row-level security so that your browser can only ever read your own records; the worker that runs searches uses separate server-side credentials that never reach the browser. Passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, and we will tell you if a breach affects you.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in (a session your browser stores) and to remember small preferences such as a dismissed checklist. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

8. International transfers

Our providers may store or process data in countries other than yours. Where that happens we rely on providers that offer recognised safeguards for international transfers (such as standard contractual clauses).

9. Children

The Service is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service grows — for example when a new alert channel or the Resume builder launches. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.

Privacy questions or requests? Contact us at support@easyy-ai.com.
